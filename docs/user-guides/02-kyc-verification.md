# How to Complete KYC Verification on Mallchain

KYC verification unlocks buying, selling, and staking. Allow 5–30 minutes for review.

## Prerequisites

- Active Mallchain account (Guide 01)
- Kenyan National ID card (plastic, not birth cert)
- Good lighting (natural daylight recommended)

## Step-by-Step KYC

1. Log in and tap the orange **Complete KYC** banner, or go to **Profile → Verification**.
   [Screenshot: Dashboard with "Complete KYC" banner and yellow "Pending" status chip]
2. Read the overview and tap **Get Started**. Select **Kenya** + **National ID**, then **Continue**.
3. Type your **Full Name** exactly as on the ID (e.g., `John Kamau Njoroge`).
   [Screenshot: Name input fields with ID example note]
4. Enter your **National ID Number** with leading zeros (e.g., `01234567`). Confirm DOB matches, tap **Next**.
5. Tap **Upload Front of ID**. Photo must show all 4 corners, no glare. Tap **Use Photo**.
   [Screenshot: ID upload screen with overlay frame guide]
6. Tap **Upload Back of ID**. Capture the barcode side. Confirm and tap **Use Photo**.
7. Tap **Take Selfie**. Position your face in the oval; avoid backlighting.
   [Screenshot: Selfie capture with oval guide and camera prompt]
8. Follow liveness prompts (smile/blink). Tap **Submit** when the selfie looks clear.
9. On the confirmation screen, look for the yellow pulsing **Pending** status chip.
   [Screenshot: Submission summary with documents listed and yellow "Pending" chip]
10. Tap **Go to Dashboard**. You'll get a push notification and email when review completes.
11. Once approved, reopen the app—the status chip changes to green **Approved** and the KYC banner disappears.
    [Screenshot: Profile screen with green "Approved" chip and checkmark]

## Troubleshooting Tips

- If rejected, the notification explains why (blurry photo, name mismatch, cropped corners). Fix the flagged item and resubmit.
