# How to Convert MallPoints to MLCN Tokens

Earn 1 MallPoint per 10 KES spent in Marketplace. Conversion rate: 100 MallPoints = 1 MLCN.

## Prerequisites

- KYC-approved account
- Unlocked MallPoints balance
- Tiny MLC for network fee (<0.01 MLC)

## Step-by-Step Conversion

1. **Dashboard** → tap the **MallPoints** card (e.g., `2,450 pts`).
   [Screenshot: Dashboard highlighting the MallPoints balance card]
2. Review breakdown (purchases, referrals, promotions). Confirm eligible unlocked points count.
3. Tap the orange **Convert to MLCN** button.
   [Screenshot: MallPoints details screen with "Convert to MLCN" button]
4. Enter MallPoints amount. Tap **Max** to use all eligible (min 100 points).
5. **MLCN You Receive** updates live (1% conversion fee already deducted).
   [Screenshot: Conversion form with Points input, MLCN output, fee breakdown]
6. Review: points deducted, MLCN received, network fee. Tap **Preview Conversion**.
7. **Signature Prompt** shows the full summary.
   [Screenshot: Signature prompt modal with summary and Sign button]
8. If correct, tap **Sign & Confirm**. Authenticate with biometrics/PIN if prompted.
9. "Broadcasting..." shows for 5–15 seconds.
10. Success banner: "Conversion complete!" with the MLCN amount.
    [Screenshot: Success screen with checkmark and "View Transaction" link]
11. Tap **View Transaction**. History shows **"Points → MLCN Conversion"** (green Confirmed badge).
12. Return to **Dashboard** → verify:
    - **MallPoints**: decreased
    - **MLCN**: increased by the net amount
    [Screenshot: Dashboard balances side-by-side after conversion]

## Quick Reference

| MallPoints | MLCN Tokens |
|---|---|
| Earned via shopping/referrals | Bought with M-Pesa or converted |
| App-only (marketplace discounts) | On-chain (send, stake, sell for KES) |

## Notes

- Min 100 points; max 50,000 points/day (basic accounts).
- Conversions are irreversible—triple-check before Sign.
- Failed Tx (e.g., low fee) does NOT deduct points.
