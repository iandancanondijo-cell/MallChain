# How to Register & Set Up Your Mallchain Wallet

Welcome to Mallchain! This guide walks you through creating your account and securing your wallet for the first time.

## Prerequisites

- A working email address (Gmail, Yahoo, or Outlook recommended)
- A smartphone or laptop with internet access
- A safe place to write down your seed phrase (notebook or password manager)

## Step-by-Step Registration

1. Open the Mallchain app or visit `https://mallchain.co.ke` in your browser.
2. Tap the **Sign Up** button on the welcome screen.
   [Screenshot: Welcome screen with Sign Up and Log In buttons]
3. Enter your email address (e.g., `juma.k@example.co.ke`) and tap **Next**.
4. Create a strong password with at least 8 characters, including one uppercase letter, one number, and one symbol like `Mallchain2024!`.
5. Re-enter the same password to confirm and tap **Create Account**.
   [Screenshot: Password creation form with strength indicator bar]
6. Check your email inbox for the verification code from Mallchain. Check the spam/junk folder if you don't see it in 2 minutes.
7. Enter the 6-digit verification code into the app and tap **Verify**.

## Back Up Your Seed Phrase (Critical!)

Your wallet comes with a 24-word seed phrase. This is the master key to your funds—**never share it with anyone**.

8. After email verification, the **Backup Wallet** screen appears. Tap **Start Backup**.
9. Write down all 24 words in the exact order shown, from Word 1 to Word 24, on a physical piece of paper.
   [Screenshot: 24 seed words displayed in a 4x6 grid with copy warning banner]
10. Store the paper in a safe, dry place away from water, fire, and prying eyes. Do NOT take a screenshot or save the words on your phone.
11. Tap **I've Written It Down**. The app will ask you to confirm 3–5 random words (e.g., "What is word number 7?").
12. Enter the correct words one by one and tap **Confirm Backup**.
    [Screenshot: Seed phrase confirmation quiz with numbered input fields]

## First Login & Dashboard Tour

13. Once backup is confirmed, you're logged in automatically. Tap through the 3 onboarding tips that explain MLCN tokens, MallPoints, and the Marketplace tab.
14. On your dashboard, you'll see your wallet address starting with `mall1...`. Tap the address to copy it to your clipboard.
    [Screenshot: Main dashboard showing wallet address, zero balances, and nav tabs at the bottom]
15. Swipe down on the dashboard to ensure balances and price feeds refresh properly.
