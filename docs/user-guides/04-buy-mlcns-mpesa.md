# How to Buy MLCN Tokens Using M-Pesa

Buy MLCN instantly via M-Pesa STK Push. Min purchase: 200 KES. Tokens arrive within 60 seconds.

## Prerequisites

- KYC-approved (green **Approved** chip)
- Safaricom M-Pesa with float + data
- Your 4-digit M-Pesa PIN ready

## Purchase Steps

1. Dashboard → tap green **Buy MLCN**, or go to **Wallet → Buy**.
   [Screenshot: Dashboard with green "Buy MLCN" button]
2. Confirm payment method is **M-Pesa (STK Push)**.
   [Screenshot: Buy page with M-Pesa selected, MLCN/KES price visible]
3. Enter KES amount (e.g., `5,000`) or tap a quick-pick button (500 / 1k / 5k / 20k).
   [Screenshot: KES input with quick-pick amount buttons]
4. **MLCN Received** field updates live (net of 2% fee). Review the exchange rate.
5. Confirm your Safaricom M-Pesa number (07 or 01 format). Edit if wrong.
   [Screenshot: M-Pesa number confirmation screen]
6. Tap **Send STK Push**.
7. Within ~10s, Safaricom pop-up: *"Mallchain Ltd requests KSh 5,000. Enter M-Pesa PIN."*
   [Screenshot: STK push prompt showing PIN input dots]
8. Wait up to 20s if delayed—don't close the app.
9. Enter 4-digit M-Pesa PIN → **OK** → **Accept** on the 2nd confirmation.
10. M-Pesa SMS confirms the deduction.
11. In Mallchain, status shows yellow **Pending** chip with spinner.
    [Screenshot: Payment status with yellow "Pending" chip]
12. Wait 10–30s until the chip turns green **Paid**.
    [Screenshot: Same screen with green "Paid" chip and checkmark]
13. MLCN credits to your wallet within 5 more seconds.
14. Open **Wallet** to verify the new balance. History shows **"M-Pesa Deposit"**.
    [Screenshot: Tx history row showing M-Pesa Deposit with green badge]

## Common Fixes

- No STK after 30s? Tap **Resend STK Push**. Check line data & M-Pesa limits.
- Money deducted, no MLCN? Refresh wallet after 2 min. Still missing? Tap **Report Issue** + share the M-Pesa SMS.
- Wrong PIN? Retry carefully—3 wrong PINs lock the SIM temporarily.
