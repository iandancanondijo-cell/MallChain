# How to Send MLCNs to Another Wallet

Txs confirm in under 10 seconds. Always double-check the address—sends are irreversible.

## Prerequisites

- MLCN/MLC in available (unstaked) balance
- Recipient address: mainnet `mall1...` or testnet `malltest1...`
- Enough MLC for network fee (0.003–0.02 MLC)

## Send Steps

1. **Wallet** tab → tap **Send** (paper-airplane icon) next to your balance card.
   [Screenshot: Wallet tab with Send/Receive buttons next to MLCN & MLC balances]
2. Choose token: **MLCN (Mainnet)** or **MLC (Testnet)** — match recipient's network.
3. Fill recipient address:
   - **Address Book**: tap 📖 → select saved contact ("Achieng - Shopkeeper").
   - **Paste**: copy address first, long-press → **Paste**.
     [Screenshot: Recipient field with address-book icon and paste menu]
4. **Triple-check** it starts with `mall1...` (or `malltest1...`). Anything else = invalid.
5. (Optional) Tap ⭐ to save address with nickname (e.g., "Mama Njeri Kiosk").
   [Screenshot: Save-to-address-book popup with nickname input]
6. (Optional) Add **Memo** like "Rent June" — public on-chain; never put PINs.
7. Tap **Next** → enter amount (e.g., `250.50`); use **50%** or **Send All** buttons.
   [Screenshot: Amount screen with Send All / 50% quick buttons]
8. Tap **Next** → choose **Fee** tier: Low (0.003, slow), Medium (0.008, 5–10s) ← rec, High (0.020, instant).
   [Screenshot: 3 fee cards; Medium selected; Total = Amount + Fee]
9. Confirm balance covers Amount + Fee → **Next**.
10. Confirmation shows recipient (truncated), amount, memo, fee, total deducted.
    [Screenshot: Send confirmation summary with Sign button]
11. (Optional) Toggle **Verify Recipient** to reveal full address — compare character-by-character.
12. Tap **Sign & Broadcast**. Authenticate with biometrics/PIN if prompted.
13. "Broadcasting..." (3–10s).
14. Green checkmark: **"Transaction Sent!"** + long **Tx Hash** appears.
    [Screenshot: Success screen with checkmark, Tx Hash, Copy button]
15. Tap **Copy Tx Hash** → share with recipient via WhatsApp/SMS if needed.
16. Tap **Done** → refresh **Wallet**; confirm balance decreased by amount + fee.
17. In **Transaction History**, a **"Send"** row with red arrow and green **Confirmed** badge.
    [Screenshot: Tx history showing Send row with Confirmed badge]

## Quick Fixes

- Low fee? Pick lower tier or get more MLC from faucet (Guide 03).
- Stuck Pending? Wait 2 min → use **Speed Up** in Tx detail to re-broadcast.
- Invalid address? Ask recipient to re-send—likely a typo or extra space.
