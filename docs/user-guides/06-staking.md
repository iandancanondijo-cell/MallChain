# How to Stake MLCN Tokens & Earn Rewards

Lock MLCN with a validator and earn ~12–18% APY. Rewards accrue daily.

## Prerequisites

- KYC-approved account
- Available (unstaked) MLCN balance
- Tiny MLC for fee (<0.01 MLC)
- **CRITICAL**: Unstaking = mandatory 14-day unbonding period (no exceptions)

## Step-by-Step Staking

1. Tap the **Staking** tab (shield icon) on the bottom nav.
   [Screenshot: Bottom nav bar with Staking icon highlighted]
2. Review top stats: **Staked**, **Earned**, **Avg APY**.
   [Screenshot: Staking dashboard stats card]
3. Browse the **Validators** list (name, commission %, APY %).
   [Screenshot: Validators list showing 5 entries with Stake buttons]
4. Choose a validator: aim APY 12–18%, commission 5–10%, uptime 99%+.
5. Tap a validator's **Stake** button (or open profile → **Stake with This Validator**).
6. Stake form opens. Enter MLCN amount; use **50%** or **Max** quick-fills (min 10 MLCN).
   [Screenshot: Stake form with validator info and MLCN amount input]
7. Review estimated daily/monthly rewards and validator commission cut.
8. Tap **Review Stake**.
9. Confirmation summary appears. Read the red banner: **"WARNING: 14-day unbonding on unstake — funds locked."**
    [Screenshot: Stake confirmation with red unbonding warning banner]
10. Tap **Sign & Stake**. Authenticate if prompted.
11. "Broadcasting..." (5–20s) → green checkmark **"Stake Successful!"**
12. Tap **View Transaction** → history shows "Delegate" (green Confirmed badge).
    [Screenshot: Tx detail showing "Delegate" type and validator]
13. Return to **Staking** → **Staked** figure now shows your locked MLCN.
14. In **My Delegations**, a row shows validator, amount, and green **Active** chip.
    [Screenshot: My Delegations listing with Active chip and rewards tracker]
15. Rewards start within minutes. Tap **Claim All** later to move rewards to your available balance (re-stake for compounding).

## Unstaking Rule

16. To unstake: **Staking → My Delegations → Your Row → Unstake**. Enter amount, confirm.
17. Status changes to **Unbonding** with 14-day countdown.
    [Screenshot: Delegation row showing "Unbonding" and 14-day timer]
18. During unbonding: no rewards, no cancel, no send/sell.
19. After exactly 14 days, tokens auto-return to available balance.

## Next Steps

- Claim & re-stake weekly for compound earnings.
- Split large stakes across 2–3 validators to reduce risk.
