# How to Sell MLCN Tokens for KES (M-Pesa Payout)

Sell MLCN → KES via M-Pesa B2C to your Safaricom line. Arrives 1–10 minutes (business hours 7am–10pm EAT).

## Prerequisites

- KYC-approved (green **Approved** chip)
- MLCN in available (unstaked) balance
- Safaricom line registered under your KYC name

## Sell Steps

1. **Wallet** tab → tap red **Sell** button next to MLCN balance.
   [Screenshot: Wallet tab with red Sell button next to MLCN balance]
2. Confirm payout method: **M-Pesa B2C**. Today's sell rate + 2% fee shown.
   [Screenshot: Sell page header "Sell MLCN → KES via M-Pesa B2C"]
3. Enter **MLCN to sell**. Use 25% / 50% / 75% / Max quick-picks.
   [Screenshot: Sell amount input with percentage quick-picks + KES preview]
4. **You Receive (KES)** updates live. Review Gross → 2% Fee → **Net Payout**.
5. Min 20 MLCN; max 50,000 MLCN/Tx (split larger sales). Tap **Continue**.
6. Confirm **M-Pesa Payout Number**. Must match your KYC-registered line.
   [Screenshot: Payout confirmation with pre-filled 07XXXXXXX number]
7. If outdated, tap **Edit**. If under different name → update KYC via Support.
8. Note timing: 1–10 min business hours; after 10pm → 7:30am next day.
9. Check both: ✅ Payout correct & in my name; ✅ Txs are irreversible.
10. Tap **Confirm Sale**.
11. Signature prompt shows final summary (MLCN, net KES, fee, payout number).
    [Screenshot: Sell summary signature prompt with Sign button]
12. Last chance: **Back** to edit, or **Sign & Submit** to proceed.
13. Processing: Step 1 Token burn (10–20s) → Step 2 B2C payout (1–5 min).
    [Screenshot: 2-step progress bar; Step 1 complete, Step 2 spinning]
14. Step 1 done → MLCN balance drops. Status: orange **"Sending KES..."**.
15. Within 1–10 min, Safaricom SMS arrives: `M-PESA Confirmed. Received Ksh43,218 from MALLCHAIN LIMITED...`
16. App status turns green **"Paid"** the moment B2C lands.
    [Screenshot: Success screen with green "Paid" chip + B2C reference]
17. Tap **Got It**.

## Check History + Fixes

18. **Wallet → History** → filter → **Sells** to view cash-outs.
    [Screenshot: History filter open; "Sells" checkbox selected]
19. Each sell row: date, MLCN sold, net KES, masked phone (`07XXXX78`), **Completed** badge, B2C ref.
20. Tap row → full receipt with B2C ref (for Safaricom follow-ups).
21. Still "Sending KES..." after 15 min? → Tx → **Report Issue** (<30 min reply).
22. App says "Paid" but no SMS? → Dial *334# to check M-Pesa balance.
23. After 10pm sale? → **"Queued — Next business batch"** with countdown.
24. Name mismatch reject? → Line must be in your KYC name; add family via Beneficiaries.
