# How to Get Free MLC Testnet Coins from the Faucet

The Testnet Faucet gives free MLC coins (100 per drip) to practice sending, staking, and paying fees without real money.

## Prerequisites

- Any Mallchain account (KYC not required)
- Wallet address visible on dashboard (starts with `mall1...`)
- Internet connection

## Claim a Drip

1. Log in and tap the **Faucet** tab (water-drop icon) on the bottom nav.
   [Screenshot: Bottom nav bar with Faucet icon highlighted]
2. The faucet page shows your `mall1...` address auto-filled. Verify it matches your dashboard address.
   [Screenshot: Faucet page with address field and "Claim 100 MLC" button]
3. Complete the captcha checkbox if shown ("I'm not a robot").
   [Screenshot: Captcha checkbox next to address field]
4. Tap **Claim 100 MLC**. The button greyes out and shows "Sending...".
5. A success toast appears: "Drip request submitted! Allow ~30 seconds for confirmation."

## Wait for Confirmation

6. Tap **View Transaction** or go to **Wallet → Transaction History**. The row shows an orange clock **Pending** badge.
   [Screenshot: Transaction history with "Faucet Drip" row and orange "Pending" badge]
7. Wait 30–60 seconds, then pull down to refresh.
8. The badge changes to a green **Confirmed** checkmark when finalized.
   [Screenshot: Same transaction with green "Confirmed" badge and block number]

## Check Your Balance

9. Go to **Dashboard** or **Wallet**. Two balances appear:
   - **MLC (Testnet)**: Now shows 100.0000 MLC
   - **MLCN (Mainnet)**: Still 0.00 until you buy (Guide 04)
   [Screenshot: Wallet balances showing 100 MLC / 0 MLCN]
10. Tap the MLC row to see available/staked/unbonding breakdowns.

## Rate Limits & Next Steps

11. The faucet button shows a 24-hour countdown until your next drip.
    [Screenshot: Greyed faucet button with "Next drip in 23h 47m" text]
12. One drip per wallet per day. If you need more quickly, ask a friend to send you MLC via Guide 07.
13. Practice sending (Guide 07), then staking (Guide 06) with your testnet coins.
14. When comfortable, buy real MLCN tokens via M-Pesa in Guide 04.
